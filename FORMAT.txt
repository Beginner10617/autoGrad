Format should be binary, all data is of 8 byte width, except activation which is 4 byte
Weights are double, everything else is unsigned (except weights of course)

<num-of-inputs> <num-of-layers> <num_of_outputs[num-of-layers]>
FOR i IN [0,<num-of-layers>)
  <activation>
  FOR j IN [0, <num_of_outputs[i]>)
    <bias-data>
    FOR k IN [0, i ? <num_of_outputs[i-1]> : <num-of-inputs>)
      <weight-data>
    END FOR
  END FOR
END FOR
